Common questions

Language and internet access

Choose Korean or English in Settings. Reading content and illustrations are included in the installation and work offline. There are no accounts, advertisements, behavioral analytics, in-app purchases, or purchase restoration. Web information and email require internet access and follow the relevant external services' policies.

If reminders do not arrive

Enable the reading reminder in the app's Settings, then check notification permission in device settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Denying permission does not prevent reading or taking notes.

Reading position, notes, and deletion

Records and settings are saved in app-specific storage. Uninstalling deletes the app's current data on that device. Restoration, retention, and deletion in existing iCloud or Google backups follow the relevant service and device settings. Records deleted in the app may remain in older backups.
