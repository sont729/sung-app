Common questions

TestFlight beta

During the TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash data and usage information and shares it with the developer; you cannot opt out of this collection. Feedback is optional and is shared with the developer when submitted. Your name and email may also be shared with the developer depending on the invitation method. Apple states that it retains feedback for one year and crash and usage information for as long as necessary to resolve bugs. See the official Apple notice. The developer’s actual receipt, access, retention, and deletion operations are still being checked. The 90-day support-email standard above does not automatically apply to TestFlight data or Apple’s processing.

If reminders do not arrive

Turn on the reading reminder in Settings, then check that notifications for 태초에 are allowed in your device settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device rather than sent from a server.

Reading position and notes

Your reading position, saved verses, notes, and settings are held in app-specific storage. Uninstalling the app deletes its current data on that device. If you use device backup, restoration and retention follow Apple or Google's backup policies and settings. Deleting a record in the app does not immediately remove it from existing backups; check the relevant service and device settings to delete backup copies.

Internet access

Reading content is included in the installation, so you can read offline. The app has no function that sends your reading position or notes to a developer server. Selecting the public Privacy Policy or Support link in Settings opens that HTTPS address in the operating system's external browser. Opening external pages or sending support email requires an internet connection. Browsers, web hosting, email services, and apps you choose as sharing destinations handle data under their own policies. Unlike reading records stored on your device, content you directly send in a support email reaches support staff.
