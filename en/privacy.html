1. Reading, notes, and personal data

The app does not provide a function that sends your reading position, saved verses, highlights, or notes to a developer server. It does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, location, or advertising identifier for reading or notes, and it does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

Support emails you choose to send, operating-system backups and diagnostics, sharing, and access to external websites are handled separately as described below. Saying that the app does not collect reading data does not mean that no information can ever leave the device by any route.

2. Records stored on your device and deletion

Your reading position, saved verses, highlights, notes, text size, theme, reading mode, language setting, and reminder time are stored in app-specific storage. The developer has no function for viewing these records remotely.

You can delete notes and highlights in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its current data on that device. If operating-system backup is enabled, records may be included in iCloud or Google device backups or device transfers. Backup, restoration, and retention or deletion of backup copies follow the relevant service and device settings. Deleting a record in the app does not immediately remove it from existing backups.

3. Optional reading reminder

The once-a-day reading reminder is off by default. The app asks for permission when you enable it in Settings and schedules it on your device. The reminder does not come from a developer server. You can still read and take notes if you deny permission.

4. Sharing and saving images that you choose

Choosing Export opens the operating system's share sheet. You choose the share card and its destination; further handling follows the policy of the app or service you select. The app does not automatically send your notes.

The app does not read your photo library. If you choose to save an image in the share sheet, the operating system may ask for permission to add photos. Adding or saving a photo follows the action you choose.

5. Operating-system diagnostics

The app has no independent crash-reporting or behavioral-analytics tools. Apple or Google may, however, process diagnostic or crash information or provide it to the developer according to device and store settings. The app does not guarantee the exact contents of that information. Please check the operating system's settings for the scope of diagnostic sharing and whether it is enabled.

During the TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash data and usage information and shares it with the developer; you cannot opt out of this collection. Feedback is optional and is shared with the developer when submitted. Your name and email may also be shared with the developer depending on the invitation method. Apple states that it retains feedback for one year and crash and usage information for as long as necessary to resolve bugs. See the official Apple notice. The developer’s actual receipt, access, retention, and deletion operations are still being checked. The 90-day support-email standard below does not automatically apply to TestFlight data or Apple’s processing.
