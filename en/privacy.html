TestFlight beta information · Formal policy under review

This information covers the free Korean/English Genesis edition in TestFlight. The formal Privacy Policy remains under review; legal approval and verification of actual support-mail operations are not complete. The displayed update date is not the effective date of a formal policy.

1. Reading and records on your device

Your reading position, saved verses, highlights, notes, text size, brightness, view mode, language, and reminder settings are stored in app-specific storage. The app has no function that sends these records to a developer server or lets the developer view them remotely.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or advertising identifier for reading or notes. There is no account registration, advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking tool, or in-app purchase. Support email, operating-system backups and diagnostics, sharing you choose, and external web access are separate. This does not mean that no information can ever leave your device by any route.

2. Deletion and backups

You can delete notes and highlights in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its current data on that device. If device backup is enabled, records may be included in iCloud or Google device backups or device transfers. Restoration, retention, and deletion of backup copies follow the relevant service and device settings. Deleting a record in the app does not immediately remove it from existing backups.

3. Optional reminders, sharing, and photos

Reading reminders are off by default. Enabling them in Settings requests permission and schedules reminders on your device, not on a developer server. Denying permission does not prevent reading or taking notes.

Export opens the operating system's share sheet. You choose the card and its destination; subsequent handling follows the selected service's policy. The app does not automatically send your notes or read your photo library. Choosing to save an image in the share sheet may prompt the operating system to request permission to add photos.

4. Operating-system and TestFlight diagnostics

The app has no independent crash-reporting or behavioral-analytics tools. Ordinary operating-system diagnostics follow Apple or Google's device and store settings; the app cannot guarantee the exact contents of that information.

Separately, when you test through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider. You cannot opt out of this collection; your name and email may be included depending on how you were invited. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared with Apple and the app provider. See Apple's TestFlight privacy information. Responsibility, access, retention, and deletion of the app provider's testing data remain under verification. The support-email standard of 90 days described below does not automatically apply to this data.

5. Support email you choose to send

If you contact dmzk2557075@gmail.com, your sender address, message, and attachments you choose to include are used to reply and troubleshoot, not for advertising or behavioral analytics. Please send only your app and operating-system versions, the affected chapter and verse or error, and steps to reproduce it. Do not send personal faith-counseling details, the original text of notes, or another person's personal data. Redact personal data in screenshots.

The operator has adopted a standard of retaining support material for 90 days after resolution and then deleting it. A procedure is being prepared to record the resolution date and include operator-controlled emails, attachments, and separately saved troubleshooting copies in the deletion scope. The responsible person, account access, deletion procedure, and test results have not yet been verified. The scope and period of any legally required retention need separate confirmation. Immediate deletion of every copy, including backups independently managed by the mail service, cannot be guaranteed.

6. Requests about support material

You can send requests for access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of support material to the support email. Identify the right you wish to exercise and the inquiry concerned. A procedure is being prepared to check identity using only minimum information, such as the original sender address and inquiry identifiers, and to verify a representative's authority using only what is necessary. Do not send a resident registration number or a copy of an identity document in advance. The responsible person and actual process for replying with the outcome or a reason for restricting a request have not yet been operationally verified.

7. External services and unresolved details

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the support address uses Gmail. GitHub's official Pages documentation states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Web hosting, browsers, and email services handle data under their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

The legal basis for handling inquiries, the roles and actual contracts of email and web services, outsourcing, and international transfers, including countries and recipients, remain unverified. Actual service and contract information and legal review are required before the formal policy is finalized. This beta information does not confirm those details or establish legal compliance.

The app's privacy and support links open an external browser without appending your reading position or notes to the web address. Unlike reading records on your device, content you directly send by support email reaches the support recipient.
