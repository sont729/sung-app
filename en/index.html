태초에 · Genesis in Korean and English, with Hebrew

Beta information · Formal policy and mail operations under review

태초에 is a reading app for one book: Genesis. All 50 chapters and 1,533 verses are included in Korean and English, alongside the Hebrew text, commentary, and scene narratives. Choose the language in Settings.

Genesis is free in both languages. There are no accounts, advertisements, or behavioral analytics. This free edition has no in-app purchase or purchase restoration.

Reading position and notes are not sent to a developer server. The Privacy Policy separately explains device backups, sharing you initiate, support email, and access to web pages.
