TestFlight beta information · Formal policy under review

This beta contains one free book: Genesis. Read all 50 chapters and 1,533 verses in Korean or English, alongside the Hebrew text, commentary, and scene narratives. Choose the language in Settings. This is not the entire Bible or a six-book edition.

There is no account registration, advertising, behavioral analytics, in-app purchase, or purchase restoration. Reading content and illustrations are included in the installation and work offline. Web pages and email need an internet connection.
